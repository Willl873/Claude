## II. The Three Most Important Changes This Quarter

**1. Gross margin inflected from ~3% to ~16% — the single most important number in this print.**
This is the first quarter where the Lanteris acquisition (closed Jan 2026) shows up in the P&L, and it appears to be doing exactly what management promised: shifting the revenue mix toward higher-margin national security/satellite work (27% of Q1 revenue) and away from low-margin, cost-plus lunar lander contracts. If this holds, it materially changes the investment case from "revenue growth story with no economics" to "revenue growth story with improving economics." One quarter is not a trend — but it's the first hard evidence the bull case (vertical integration → margin expansion) is showing up in numbers, not just slides.

**2. Revenue pacing implies a steep back-half ramp is required to hit guidance.**
Q1 revenue of $186.7M annualizes to ~$747M — below the $900M–$1B FY26 guidance range. Management reiterated guidance unchanged, which mathematically requires **~$254M/quarter average for the remaining three quarters** (vs. $186.7M in Q1), a ~36% sequential step-up. Management attributes this to backlog conversion timing (Lanteris backlog recognition, Andromeda IDIQ ramp) rather than a shortfall — but this is an assumption to track, not a fact yet confirmed.

**3. Cash fell 60% quarter-over-quarter ($582.6M → $231.6M) while GAAP net loss was $37.4M — the cash use exceeds the P&L loss.**
The delta is explained by the $450M cash portion of the Lanteris acquisition consideration, not by operating burn alone — this is a one-time M&A cash outflow, not a recurring burn rate. But it means the cash cushion that existed at year-end 2025 is now much thinner, and the **~$500M ATM equity program** disclosed around this period is the mechanism management has set up to refill it. Track dilution from here.

The core bear-case mechanism (cash burn/dilution) also showed up, largely as previously flagged. Net effect: the thesis is **more testable now, not more resolved** — one data point is not a trend, but it is directionally the first confirming data point for the optimistic scenario in the prior report.

**3. Next catalyst to watch:**
Q2 2026 earnings — specifically (a) whether the ~$254M/quarter pace materializes to keep FY26 guidance intact, (b) whether the ~$20M "nonrecurring" SG&A actually normalizes down as promised, and (c) whether orbital/trade receivables growth decelerates relative to revenue.

**4. Action if already holding:**
**Hold, do not add on this print alone.** The margin inflection is encouraging but is one quarter of a still-unresolved integration, riding on a revenue base that is majority acquisition-driven rather than organic. Wait for Q2 to confirm the margin trend is durable and the receivables issue resolves before treating this as a structural re-rating event.
