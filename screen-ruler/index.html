How to use

  1. Calibrate once. Tap Calibrate and match the outline to a credit card so the ruler shows true size.
  2. Measure. Line your object up with the ruler, then drag markers A and B to its ends. The distance shows below.
  3. Be precise. Click anywhere on the ruler to jump the nearest marker there, or focus a marker and use the arrow keys (hold Shift for bigger steps).
  4. Switch it up. Toggle cm/inch, flip between horizontal and vertical, and turn on Snap for whole-millimetre steps.

Your calibration is saved on this device, so you only do it once per screen.
